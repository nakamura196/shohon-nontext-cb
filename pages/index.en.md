This companion site provides visual browsing and verification for our dataset of text-presence and imaging-target labels across all 76,381 page images (IIIF canvases) of 714 witnesses of five Japanese classical works (*Ise monogatari*, *Kokin wakashū*, *Makura no sōshi*, *Taketori monogatari*, and *Tsurezuregusa*) in the [NIJL Union Catalogue Database of Japanese Texts (Kokusho Database)](https://kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/?ln=en).

To keep browsing focused on non-text pages and audit samples rather than ordinary body-text pages, this collection includes **all 3,620 images labelled as having no text**, together with **all 318 images checked by the author (Stage 3)**, **all 300 images judged independently by the second annotator (Stage 4)**, and **21 legend example images** (**4,131 canvases** in total).

No image files are hosted here; all thumbnails and full images are loaded directly via the Kokusho Database IIIF Image API. Use [Browse & Filter](browse.html) or [Search](search/) to filter by text presence, page type, imaging targets (ruler, colour chart, illustration), or verification set (including every label corrected by the author in Stage 3).
